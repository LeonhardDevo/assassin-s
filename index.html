Memory Sequence 05 — The Lore

The Creed

For centuries, two secret orders have shaped the fate of humanity from the shadows.

style

Stay Your Blade

Never harm the innocent. The Assassin's blade is a tool of precision, not of mindless slaughter — discipline is the mark of a true master.

visibility

Always Be Discreet

Blend with the crowd. Strike from the shadows. The Assassin walks among the people, unseen until the moment of truth arrives.

shield

Never Compromise the Brotherhood

The order endures beyond any single life. Its secrets, its members, and its purpose must be protected at all costs — even one's own.

The Assassins

The Hidden Ones

The Assassin Brotherhood, originally founded as the Hidden Ones by Bayek of Siwa in ancient Egypt, fights for the freedom of humanity. They believe that free will — even flawed — is superior to ordered subjugation.

Through the centuries, legendary figures such as Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad, Ezio Auditore, Connor Kenway, and Eivor Varinsdottir have carried the Creed forward.

The Templars

The Order of the Knights

The Templar Order believes that humanity cannot be trusted with its own freedom. They seek to guide civilization through order and control, using Isu artifacts — the Pieces of Eden — to shape the course of history.

In the modern era, the Templars operate through Abstergo Industries, a global corporation that develops the Animus technology to locate Isu artifacts through genetic memory.

auto_awesomeThe Isu

A precursor civilization that preceded humanity. The Isu created the Pieces of Eden and engineered Homo sapiens as a servant species. Their civilization collapsed in a great cataclysm, but their artifacts endure.

deployed_codePieces of Eden

Powerful Isu artifacts — Apples of Eden, Shrouds, Staffs, Swords — capable of manipulating minds, healing, and projecting illusions. Both Assassins and Templars seek them to tip the balance of their eternal war.

memoryThe Animus

A machine that reads genetic memory, allowing users to relive the experiences of their ancestors. Originally developed by Abstergo, it is the bridge between past and present — and the gateway through which history becomes our playground.
