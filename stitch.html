History is Our Playground

For over a decade, the Assassin's Creed franchise has taken players on breathtaking journeys through the pivotal moments of human history. From the sun-drenched sands of ancient Egypt to the bustling streets of Renaissance Florence, and the mist-shrouded fjords of the Viking age.

Join the ancient order of Assassins in their eternal struggle against the Templars, fighting for the freedom of humanity across centuries.
